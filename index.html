Storage

Solar interconnection follows NEC 705.12 (120% busbar rule) or a supply-side tap. Confirm against the actual busbar and main.

Interconnection (NEC 705.12 / 705.13)

Smart Panel busbar is 225 A. 120% rule: main + backfeed ≤ 1.2 × busbar. Multiple battery systems on separate points of connection are evaluated per point per NEC 705.12, subject to AHJ. SPAN's Power Control System (705.13) can hold combined current to the busbar and remove the 120% limit. Confirm against the actual equipment and utility/AHJ rules.
